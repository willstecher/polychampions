The Novas are the rookie team where all new league players start. In order to join the Novas, you must register your Polytopia in-game name in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/7AKFVrZfqb) server. To access multiplayer in the app, you must purchase at least one tribe.
 To graduate from the Novas and be eligible to get on a team, a player must complete at least one 2v2 game and start at least one more.
