**PolyELO **is matchmaking and ranking system to help players find other people to play multiplayer Polytopia games. It uses an [Elo rating system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elo_rating_system) so that game winners will gain PolyElo points, and losers will lose points.  Results from ranked/competitive games will affect players' Elo score and ranking on the leader boards. Unranked/casual games do not affect ELO.

To use this system, a player must:

- Register your Player ID on the official [Polytopia Discord server](http://discord.gg/yWCx98Z)
- Enter the game details into the PolyElo bot
- Declare the winner into the PolyElo bot after the game is finished

More details on the PolyElo bot commands are available in the Multiplayer-Elo-Guide on the [main discord server.](http://discord.gg/yWCx98Z)
