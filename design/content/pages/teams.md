## Dragons

Dragons are a high-performing and diverse house, incorporating top players across a wide age range. We finished top seed in the last two seasons (S15 and 16). Characterized by stellar communication coupled with impressive game-sense, we’re a dedicated community of tryhards, waging war to the best of our abilities. If you’ll put in the effort to become the very best, then we’ll gladly add you to our roster.

## Kraken

House Kraken is a competitive and friendly team full of both new and experienced players. Our house offers new members an environment to learn the game while having fun playing in PolyChampions. House Kraken provides two encouraging and helpful teams for both old and new players to enjoy on their Polytopia journey. Come join the Krakens and Squids under the waves in The Trench’s murky depths!

![Kraken](https://polychampions.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/kraken-border.png)

## Lightning

![Lightning ](https://polychampions.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/LightningLogo2.png)

House Lightning, rich in history and legend, has built a system that produces wins and great players. We draft passionate players who love the game, seek improvement, and enjoy a friendly community. Our team is run by leadership consensus, with all players involved in major decisions. Active players interested in leadership get opportunities to move up. We have two strong teams, Lightning and Thundercats, and a new third team, Electrons, for newer players to learn and grow.

## Plague

Plague is the House for you if you can blend competition with a chill vibe. We are always looking for competitive and enthusiastic members. We conduct many activities like in-house tournaments, training games, poly puzzles, online Catan, and question/song of the day. We have an entire group of advisors ready to guide you in your games and throughout your Polytopia career. We’re an energetic, competitive, and enthusiastic community, and we would love to have you aboard!

![Plague](https://polychampions.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/plague-border.png)
