## Season 7

![](https://polychampions.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Season-7-champs-Craw-1.png)

## Season 6

Congratulations to The [Sparkies ](https://polychampions.com/sparkies/)for winning the Season 6 playoffs vs the Jets!

## Season 5

The [Lightning ](https://polychampions.com/lightning/)has emerged victorious and won pro season 5! After dazzling 14-4 results in the normal season they then crushed the Wildfire 2-1 in the semifinals and schooled the plague 2-1 in the finals.

## Season 4

Congratulations to The [Crawfish ](https://polychampions.com/crawfish/)for winning the Season 4! With teams core players retiring, including leaders Hughmungus and Koric, and with growth of some talented Shrimps team will undergo huge changes.

## Season 3

The Season 3 Champions has been cemented in Polytopia and PolyChampions history as The [Crawfish](https://polychampions.com/crawfish/)!  The [Jets ](https://polychampions.com/jets/)have made their 3rd straight appearance in the final, but fell short to the expansion team known as the Crawfish

![](https://polychampions.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Season-4-champs-Craw-1-1024x1024.png)
