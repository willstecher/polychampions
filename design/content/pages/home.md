## Welcome

![](https://polychampions.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Battle-of-Polytopia-400.jpg.png)

PolyChampions is a competitive league for the strategy game [The Battle of Polytopia](http://midjiwan.com/polytopia.html). Matches are organized through the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/7AKFVrZfqb). The league focuses on competitive, team-based, organized gameplay. We promote sportsmanship, tolerance, and a positive attitude. We also promote training, tactics, and education in the world of Polytopia.

## How the League Works

Teams play 12 games per season, one 2v2 and one 3v3 game vs every other team in their division. Teammates share screenshots of their game and coordinate moves to plan a joint strategy. The top teams in each division are promoted and the bottom teams are relegated. 

All matches are assigned and wins logged in our Discord server

## Get on a Team

To join one of the [15 league houses](https://polychampions.com/teams/), find the league on [discord](https://discord.gg/7AKFVrZfqb), pass probation as a [Nova](https://polychampions.com/novas/), and contact a recruiter.

Read [step by step instructions](https://polychampions.com/novas/) for how to join the league.

## League History

First [founded in May, 2018](https://polychampions.com/history/) with players placed into 4 color groups, PolyChampions games are now divided into seasons, consisting of 2 vs 2 and 3 vs 3 matches, organized by team brackets. 

Today, we have over 25 teams, each competing at different skill levels. Seasons last three months. Each team has around 5-20 members.

The league is now in its 21st season and is actively recruiting new members.
